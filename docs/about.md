# About me

I'm Sawyer Greaves, a robotics graduate student at the University of Utah. My interests center on robotics, control systems, and building robots to explore space. I'm applying for PhD positions where I can continue developing these interests through research.

My work in the [Magnetic and Medical Robotics Lab](https://mmrobotics.mech.utah.edu/) includes simulation and control for magnetic manipulation in space. I also enjoy making physical systems: designing mechanisms, building circuits, writing control software, and bringing those pieces together.

## Research and engineering

My [graduate research](./research/) focuses on magnetic manipulation and simulation. My [projects](./projects/) span camera tracking, autonomous robots, inverse kinematics, and scientific visualization.

Across that work, I've used Python, C++, microcontrollers, CAD, and 3D printing. I enjoy the connection between a mathematical idea and a robot that can demonstrate it.
