# Five-degree-of-freedom robot arm

**Personal project · July–December 2024**

I built a five-degree-of-freedom robot arm with a gripper. My focus was the circuit design and the connection between the electronics and the physical arm.

![A five-degree-of-freedom robot arm with a gripper.](/images/robot-arm.webp)

## Design and implementation

The project involved circuit design, C++, control systems, CAD, and 3D printing.

Most of the mechanical design came from [HowToMechatronics' robot arm project](https://howtomechatronics.com/tutorials/arduino/diy-arduino-robot-arm-with-smartphone-control/). My work focused on building the system and developing its circuitry.
