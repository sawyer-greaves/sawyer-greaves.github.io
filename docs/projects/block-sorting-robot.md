# Autonomous block-sorting robot

**University of Utah · Team of four · August–December 2024**

As part of a four-person team, I helped design, manufacture, and program an autonomous robot for the University of Utah's mechanical engineering robotics competition.

![The autonomous block-collecting and sorting robot built for a University of Utah engineering competition.](/images/block-sorting-robot.webp)

## The challenge

The robot had to navigate the competition arena, collect and sort colored blocks, and deliver them to a home position without manual control.

## Building the system

We integrated the robot's mechanical design, electronics, and control software as a team. The project involved CAD, 3D printing, circuit design, microcontrollers, and C++ programming, with the competition providing a practical test of how those systems worked together.
