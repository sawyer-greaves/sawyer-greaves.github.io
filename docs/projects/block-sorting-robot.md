# Autonomous block-sorting robot

**University of Utah · Team of four · August–December 2024**

Our team designed, manufactured, and programmed an autonomous robot for the University of Utah's mechanical engineering robotics competition.

![The autonomous block-collecting and sorting robot built for a University of Utah engineering competition.](/images/block-sorting-robot.webp)

## The challenge

The robot had to navigate a dynamic environment, collect and sort colored blocks, and deliver those blocks to a home position.

## Building the system

The project brought together hardware design, control system design, circuit design, CAD, 3D printing, microcontrollers, and C++ programming. We developed the robot as a team, integrating its mechanical and electronic systems with the software needed for autonomous operation.
