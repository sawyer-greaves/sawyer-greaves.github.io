# Camera tracking with a mechanical differential

**Personal project · June–September 2025**

I designed, manufactured, and assembled a mechanical differential with a mounted camera, then connected it to an object-tracking feedback loop.

![A CAD rendering of a camera-mounted mechanical differential, showing its gears, motors, and electronics.](/images/differential-camera.webp)

## How it works

The camera streams live video wirelessly to my laptop. Tracking software follows a selected object in the image, and the laptop sends wireless motor commands to adjust the camera's orientation and keep the object in view.

## What I worked on

My work covered mechanical design and fabrication, circuit design, microcontroller programming, image tracking, and control software. The project gave me experience connecting a mechanism I had built to feedback from a camera.
