# Mechanical differential with camera

**Personal project · June–September 2025**

I designed, manufactured, and assembled a mechanical differential, then mounted a camera on it to create a mechanism with a wide potential viewing area.

![A CAD rendering of a camera-mounted mechanical differential, showing its gears, motors, and electronics.](/images/differential-camera.webp)

## How it works

The camera streams live video wirelessly to my laptop. Image tracking identifies the selected object, and the laptop sends wireless commands to the motors to keep that object in the frame.

## What I worked on

This project combined mechanical design and fabrication with circuit design, microcontrollers, image tracking, and control software. It connected the physical mechanism to a feedback loop driven by the camera image.
