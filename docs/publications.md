# Research manuscripts

The manuscripts below are under review, under revision, or in preparation. Intended venues are identified for work that has not yet been submitted.

## Under review or revision

### Academy Transaction Note: Capturing Objects in Microgravity Using Induction

**S. R. Greaves**, C. R. Lambert, K. Dhekar, and J. J. Abbott.\
*Acta Astronautica* · **Under revision for resubmission**\
First and corresponding author.

This work investigates using multiple rotating magnetic sources to collect conductive objects in microgravity. I designed and conducted experiments on interactions between nearby objects, synthesized prior experiments and simulations, produced figures, and wrote methods and results sections.

[Read about the project](./research/vacuum-cleaner.md)

### Force and Torque Induced on a Conductive Nonmagnetic Cylinder by a Rotating Magnetic Dipole

L. M. Barker, T. J. Allen, **S. R. Greaves**, and J. J. Abbott.\
*IEEE Transactions on Magnetics* · **Under review**

This work develops empirical models of magnetically induced force and torque on conductive, nonmagnetic cylinders. I contributed to expanding the force–torque modeling data across cylinder geometries and dimensions.

## In preparation

### Interpolating Models of Magnetically Induced Wrenches in SO(3)

**S. R. Greaves** and J. J. Abbott.\
**In preparation** · Intended venue: *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*\
First and corresponding author.

I proposed and implemented an orientation-space interpolation method for constructing continuous magnetic-wrench models from sparse force and torque measurements, motivated by the limitations of sphere-based models for non-spherical objects.

[Read about the project](./research/orientation-interpolation.md)

### First Demonstration of Six-Degree-of-Freedom Manipulation Using Magnetic Induction Enabled by a Novel Neutral-Buoyancy Simulation of Microgravity

T. J. Allen, K. A. Ben-Jacob, R. R. Scoggan, T. C. Wilcox, **S. R. Greaves**, E. M. VanderHoeven, A. P. Newey, L. E. Martin, D. A. Wall, G. F. Tabor, and J. J. Abbott.\
**In preparation** · Intended venue: *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*

This work presents a neutral-buoyancy platform for physical six-degree-of-freedom manipulation using magnetic induction. I contributed online vision-based state estimation for the platform.

For related dynamics simulation work, see [six-degree-of-freedom dynamics simulation](./research/magnetic-manipulation.md).
