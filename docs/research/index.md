# Research

I work with Prof. Jake J. Abbott in the University of Utah's [Magnetic & Medical Robotics Lab](https://mmrobotics.mech.utah.edu/). My research concerns magnetic manipulation of electrically conductive objects: using changing magnetic fields to induce forces and torques without physical contact. The lab studies this approach for applications including object capture and space-debris manipulation.

My contributions span rigid-body dynamics simulation, experimental force and torque measurements, and modeling. A recurring question in my work is whether the assumptions in a model are adequate for the system we want to control. That question has led me to investigate interactions between nearby objects and orientation-dependent magnetic responses of non-spherical objects.
