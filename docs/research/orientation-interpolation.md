# Interpolating Models of Magnetically Induced Wrenches in SO(3)
 
**Magnetic and Medical Robotics Lab · University of Utah**  
Started August 2026

Can we determine the ideal way to interpolate between a sparse data set for magnetically induced wrenches on objects whose response is highly dependent on their relative orientation to the magnetic source?

![3D representation of the unit hypersphere with simplex outlined.](/images/orientation-interpolation.webp)

## Results and next steps

This research is still in the early phases. Early signs have been extremely positive that treating the orientation quaternions as basis vectors gives very accurate interpolation.

## Research outputs

The resulting work "Interpolating Models of Magnetically Induced Wrenches in SO(3)" is currently in preparation for submission to IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.

Related publications and preprints will be listed on the [publications page](../publications.md) as they become available.
