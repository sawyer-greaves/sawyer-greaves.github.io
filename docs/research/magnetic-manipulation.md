# Magnetic manipulation in space

**Magnetic and Medical Robotics Lab · University of Utah**  
Started May 2025

How can a magnetic field be used to manipulate a conductive object in space? My work explores this problem through simulation and control.

![A six-degree-of-freedom rigid-body simulator showing a conductive object, reference axes, and surrounding geometry.](/images/magnetic-manipulation.webp)

## The problem

The system uses the rotation of a nearby magnetic dipole to manipulate a conductive object. Controlling the object's motion requires considering six degrees of freedom: its position and orientation in three-dimensional space.

## My contribution

I developed a simulation suite in Python to test and develop control strategies for this system. The simulator provides a way to explore the object's motion and compare control approaches within a modeled environment.

This work brings together robotics, magnetics, control system design, and simulation.
